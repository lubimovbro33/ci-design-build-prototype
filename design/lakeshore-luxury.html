Design Vision

Warm Materials, Lake Light

The building’s existing infrastructure was kept, and the plan was tuned around it

Rather than approaching each room independently, the team developed a cohesive language connecting architecture, materials, lighting, furniture, art, and daily function

Warm, textured natural materials with touches of blue run through the open living, kitchen and dining space, where lighting and seating mark each zone; the kitchen was built in walnut with decorative hardware, and the owners moved into a home whose furniture already stood in place
