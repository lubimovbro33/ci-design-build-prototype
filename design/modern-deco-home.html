Design Vision

Modern Deco

The design set modern lines against deco contrast: a burnished black fireplace on white wall panels

Rather than approaching each room independently, the team developed a cohesive language connecting architecture, materials, lighting, furniture, art, and daily function

Custom detail runs through the entry and the powder room, the casings, the wood doors and the stone counter profiles, and integrated lighting is set to the art CI curated for the rooms
