11 · FAQ

11 FAQ

What Clients Want to Know Before They Begin

What makes a great client at CI?

Clients who value their time, their health, and their wellness — and want one accountable team to protect all three.

What does “turnkey” mean at CI?

At CI, turnkey means exactly that — we handle everything. Design, construction, furnishings, art, installation, and move-in, all managed by one integrated team under one roof. You walk into a finished home, not a finished renovation. From the first conversation to the night you move in, every detail is coordinated, every decision is managed, and every layer of the experience is delivered at a concierge level. One team. One standard. Complete responsibility.

How long will my project take?

Projects follow two phases: Design & Planning, then Construction. A detailed schedule is developed early and refined as decisions are finalized — realistic timelines, clear milestones.

How is CI’s pricing structured?

Transparently. Three components: pass-through costs at CI preferred pricing, a management fee based on project complexity and duration, and a clearly defined overhead and profit structure.

Can I purchase my own furniture?

Yes — however, items not procured through CI fall outside our coordination. That includes tracking, receiving, inspection, storage, delivery, installation, and any warranty or damage claims.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you want to be. We bring decisions to you at the right moment. The details in between are our responsibility.

Can we live in the home during construction?

It depends on scope. We'll advise clearly during the discovery phase.

Does CI handle permitting, approvals, and inspections?

Yes.

What types of projects does CI take on, and where?

Significant residential renovations and new builds in Chicago and South Florida.

02 · What CI Wellness Is

02 What CI Wellness Is

The Home as Part of Your Wellbeing

We spend a significant part of our lives at home.

The environments around us influence daily routines in ways both visible and invisible: the quality and rhythm of light, the air we breathe, the water we use, the acoustics we live with, the temperature we sleep in, and the rituals that help us recover.

CI Wellness brings these considerations into residential design.

It is not a medical service.

And it is not about turning your residence into a clinic, gym, or technology showcase.

It is about understanding the ways your home can better support your lifestyle and then designing those opportunities into the architecture with intention.

03 · Why Wellness Matters to CI

03 Why Wellness Matters to CI

Better Design Should Support a Better Quality of Life

Luxury has traditionally been defined by what a home contains.

Light · Air · Water · Sleep · Recovery · Acoustics · Temperature · Movement · Daily routines

These are not separate from residential design.
They are part of how a home performs.

By considering them early, wellness can influence planning, infrastructure, lighting, material selections, mechanical systems, bathrooms, bedrooms, technology, and dedicated recovery environments in ways that feel natural rather than added on.

This is how CI is redefining luxury: beyond appearance alone and toward the quality of everyday living.

05 · The Integrated Wellness Process

05 The Integrated Wellness Process

Expert Perspective. One Coordinated Team.

Wellness is personal.
The right solution should begin by understanding the individual—not by starting with a catalogue of products.

01

Understand the Client

CI begins with your lifestyle.

How you sleep. How you start and end the day. How you exercise. How you recover. How you use your bathroom and bedroom. How much control you want over lighting and technology. What currently supports you. What could work better.

These conversations help define where design can have the most meaningful impact.

02

Add a Wellness Perspective

Where appropriate, CI collaborates with Dr. Alka to bring an additional wellness perspective to the conversation.

Her perspective can help inform a deeper understanding of individual priorities and opportunities as they relate to the client's desired lifestyle and home environment.

This collaboration is intended to inform design thinking—not provide medical treatment through CI.

03

Translate Insight Into Design

CI then translates those priorities into the language of the home.

Architecture. Lighting. Mechanical planning. Bathrooms. Bedrooms. Materials. Water. Air. Technology. Acoustics. Recovery environments.

The goal is to turn an intention such as wanting more restorative sleep or building recovery into a daily routine into tangible design decisions the project team can execute.

04

Design + Coordinate the System

Wellness features often touch several disciplines at once.

A steam shower affects plumbing, waterproofing, electrical work, controls, ventilation, material selection, and construction detailing.

Circadian lighting affects fixture specification, controls, programming, electrical planning, and interior atmosphere.

Water filtration affects infrastructure before a fixture is ever selected.

CI coordinates these relationships as part of the larger project.

05

Build + Integrate

Our team manages specification, design coordination, construction, installation, vendor communication, and integration so wellness systems feel like part of the residence—not equipment added afterward.

The client does not have to manage a collection of disconnected contractors, consultants, technology companies, and product specialists.

CI remains the central point of coordination.
